What we build

Our first title is SERANTUM (세란텀: 은하 대개척), a mobile space-strategy game in which every player colonizes planets, commands fleets and competes for territory inside one shared, persistent galaxy. It launched worldwide on August 18, 2026 on Google Play and the App Store. The client is built with Unity; the server-authoritative backend runs on Supabase (Postgres) with Edge Functions for combat resolution.

Founder

Jaesik Lim spent 13 years as a software engineer at LG Electronics, moving from embedded systems to mobile applications, before founding DURU GAMES in 2026 to make the kind of strategy game he wanted to play. The studio is bootstrapped and has taken no outside investment.
